What this comparison establishes

Force and yaw equilibrium, combined tire grip, hydraulic supply, steering/sideslip bounds, and the car outline inside a local road corridor are checked at each segment's start, midpoint and end. The offline planner knows the circuit; the brake allocation algorithm uses current signals only. Playback follows a calculated profile, not a transient handling simulation.

Power, brake gains, master bores, valve settings and vehicle dimensions include research assumptions. Rate/filter effects remain in the separate timed study. Tire load sensitivity, wheel lockup recovery, roll/sideslip transients, driver confidence and competition legality are unresolved. Small lap differences require spacing and parameter-sensitivity checks.
